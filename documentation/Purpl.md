Build Google Play purchases, customer entitlement checks, restoration, and Purpl service integration with one consistent API.

Use `purpl` with only a `PurchaseConfiguration` defined in your app. Connect the Purpl service to keep the same purchase API while adding server-managed entitlements and remote paywall configuration. Add `purpl-ui` when you also want the default Jetpack Compose paywall.

Google Play always remains authoritative for product information, pricing, and purchase availability.

For guides and the Purpl service, see the [Purpl documentation](https://purpl.sh/en/docs). For Apple platforms, see the [Purpl Swift API Reference](https://swift.purpl.sh/).
